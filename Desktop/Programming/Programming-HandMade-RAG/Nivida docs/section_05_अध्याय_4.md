2. निविदाओं के मूल्यांकन हेतु निविदा की संख्या एवं एकल निविदा का निस्तारण— निविदाओं की संख्या के आधार पर निविदाओं के मूल्यांकन के संबंध में Uttar Pradesh Procurement Manual of Goods, 2016 के प्रस्तर-14.15(4) में निम्नलिखित प्रावधान किये गये हैं—

"The number of tenderers qualified in technical evaluation should not generally be less than 3 (three). If the number is less than 3 (three) and it is considered necessary by the procuring entity to continue with the bid process, reasons shall be recorded in writing and included in the record of the procurement proceedings."

**नियम एवं शर्तें:**

*   **निविदाकर्ताओं की न्यूनतम संख्या:** तकनीकी मूल्यांकन में योग्य निविदाकर्ताओं की संख्या सामान्यतः 3 (तीन) से कम नहीं होनी चाहिए।
*   **कम निविदाकर्ता होने पर प्रक्रिया:** यदि संख्या 3 (तीन) से कम है और क्रय इकाई द्वारा बोली प्रक्रिया जारी रखना आवश्यक समझा जाता है, तो इसके कारण लिखित रूप में दर्ज किए जाएंगे और खरीद कार्यवाही के रिकॉर्ड में शामिल किए जाएंगे।

**(1) एकल निविदा—**
मैनुअल फॉर प्रोक्योरमेंट ऑफ वर्क्स-2022, भारत सरकार के प्रस्तर-5.6.7 तथा ग्रामीण सड़क विकास मंत्रालय भारत सरकार के परिपत्र संख्या–पी0-17025/33/2011/पी0–1/आर0सी0 दिनांक 15 जनवरी, 2013 के बिन्दु–4(ए) में एकल निविदा को निम्नानुसार परिभाषित किया गया है।

*   **(क) प्रस्तर–5.6.7 (एकल प्रस्ताव की परिभाषा):**
    "Sometimes, against advertised/ limited tender cases, the procuring entity may not receive a sufficient number of bids and/ or after analysing the bids, ends up with **only one responsive bid** – a situation referred to as 'Single Offer'. As per Rule 21 of DFPR (explanation sub-para), such situation of 'Single Offer' is to be treated as Single Tender."

*   **(ख) बिन्दु–4(ए) (एकल निविदा की परिभाषा):**
    "Definition of Single Tender: If consequent to invitation of tenders/bids for any package, only one bid/tender is received or **consequent to technical evaluation if only one bid/tender is found eligible**, such bids/ tenders shall be termed as single tenders for the purposes of the PMGSY."

**(2) एकल निविदा का निस्तारण—**
मैनुअल फॉर प्रोक्योरमेंट ऑफ वर्क्स, 2022 भारत सरकार के प्रस्तर–5.6.7, UP Manual Procurement of Goods, 2016 के प्रस्तर–14.21 तथा लोक निर्माण विभाग, उ0प्र0 के शासनादेश 2173/तेईस–7–2018–111(स0)/2017 दिनांक 07 जनवरी, 2019 में एकल निविदा के निस्तारण की निम्नलिखित व्यवस्था की गयी है।

(25)

#### संभावी प्रश्नोत्तर

1.  **What is the minimum number of tenderers generally required to qualify in technical evaluation?**
    The number of tenderers qualified in technical evaluation should not generally be less than 3 (three).

2.  **एकल निविदा (Single Tender) को बिन्दु-4(ए) के अनुसार कैसे परिभाषित किया गया है?**
    बिन्दु-4(ए) के अनुसार, यदि निविदाओं/बोली के आमंत्रण के परिणामस्वरूप केवल एक बोली/निविदा प्राप्त होती है या तकनीकी मूल्यांकन के परिणामस्वरूप केवल एक बोली/निविदा योग्य पाई जाती है, तो ऐसी बोलियों/निविदाओं को पीएमजीएसवाई के प्रयोजनों के लिए एकल निविदा माना जाएगा।

**(क) प्रस्तर-14.21** शासनादेश 2173/तेईस-7-2018-111(स०)/2017 दिनांक 07 जनवरी, 2019 – इस शासनादेश में एकल निविदा के निस्तारण की निम्नवत् व्यवस्था दी गयी है:

*   (i) प्रथम बार में प्राप्त एकल निविदा की तकनीकी बिड न खोली जाए।
*   (ii) यदि द्वितीय बार में भी एकल निविदा प्राप्त होती है, तो पूर्ण औचित्य देते हुए सक्षम अधिकारी की अनुमति से वित्तीय बिड खोली जाए।

**(ख) प्रस्तर-14.21** Sometimes the purchase organisation may not receive a sufficient number of tenders. A situation may also arise where, after analysing the tenders, the purchase organisation ends up with one responsive tenderer. In such situations, the purchase organisation is first to check whether, while floating/issuing the tender enquiry, all necessary requirements like:

*   Standard tender enquiry conditions
*   Industry friendly specification
*   Wide publicity
*   Sufficient time for formulation of tenders
*   Qualification criteria

etc. were fulfilled. If not, the tender is to be re-issued/re-floated after rectifying the deficiencies.

1.  However, if after scrutiny it is found that all such aspects were fully taken care of, the contract for a tender value does not exceed 50 (fifty) lac and:
    *   a) the price quoted by the bidder is assessed to be reasonable;
    *   b) the bid is unconditional and complete in all respects;
    *   c) there is no obvious indicators of cartelisation amongst bidders;
    *   d) the bid evaluation committee shall prepare a justification note for the approval of the next higher authority of the procuring entity, who shall decide as to whether to sanction the single bid or re-invite bids after recording reasons.
2.  In case of contract for estimated value exceeding 50 (fifty) lac but not exceeding 5 (five) crore, the tender should be re-floated and if the purchase organisation again ends up with only one responsive tenderer, the contract may be placed on that tenderer, after ensuring the aforesaid procedure.

#### संभावी प्रश्नोत्तर

**Search Query 1:** When should a technical bid for a single tender not be opened as per the government order?
**Answer 1:** As per शासनादेश 2173/तेईस-7-2018-111(स०)/2017 दिनांक 07 जनवरी, 2019, the technical bid for a single tender received for the first time (`प्रथम बार में प्राप्त एकल निविदा की तकनीकी बिड न खोली जाए।`) should not be opened.

**Search Query 2:** What is the procedure for a single responsive tender if the estimated contract value is between 50 lac and 5 crore?
**Answer 2:** In such a case, the tender should be re-floated. If, after re-floating, the purchase organisation still receives only one responsive tenderer, the contract may be placed on that tenderer after ensuring the prescribed procedure (i.e., verifying the reasonableness of price, unconditional bid, no cartelisation, and approval from the next higher authority).

**(3) Lastly for contracts of estimated value exceeding 5 (five) crore the tender should be retendered second time and if it also results in only one responsive tender, contract may be finalised on the same basis as above. **Contracts exceeding 5 crore threshold:**

*   **Initial Scenario:** Tender estimated value exceeds 5 crore.
*   **Action 1:** Tender should be retendered a second time.
*   **Action 2:** If the second retender also results in only one responsive tender, the contract may be finalised on the same basis as above.
*   **Approval Exemption:** If the procuring entity is the administrative department of the Government, approval from the next higher authority will not be required in such cases.

**(ग) प्रस्तर—5.6.7** The contract may be placed on the ‘Single Offer’ bidder provided the quoted price is reasonable. However, restricted powers of Single tender mode of procurement would apply.

Before retendering, the procuring entity is first to check whether, while floating/ issuing the enquiry, all necessary requirements and formalities such as standard conditions, industry friendly specification, wide publicity, sufficient time for bidding, and so on, were fulfilled. If not, a fresh enquiry is to be issued after rectifying the deficiencies.

It has become a practice among some procuring entities to routinely assume that open tenders which result in single bids are not acceptable and to go for retender as a safe course of action. This is not correct.

Re-bidding has costs:
*   Firstly, the actual costs of retendering;
*   Secondly, the delay in execution of the work with consequent delay in the attainment of the purpose for which the procurement is being done;
*   And thirdly, the possibility that the re-bid may result in a higher bid.

Lack of competition shall not be determined solely on the basis of the number of bidders. Even when only one bid is submitted, the process may be considered valid provided the following conditions are satisfied:

*   i) The procurement was satisfactorily advertised and sufficient time was given for submission of bids;
*   ii) The qualification criteria were not unduly restrictive; and
*   iii) Prices are reasonable in comparison to market values.

However, restricted powers of Single tender mode of procurement would apply. In case of price not being reasonable, negotiations (being L1) or retender may be considered as justifiable.

#### संभावी प्रश्नोत्तर

1.  **Query:** Under what conditions can a single bid be considered valid even if only one bid is submitted?
    **Answer:** A single bid may be considered valid if the procurement was satisfactorily advertised with sufficient time for submission, qualification criteria were not unduly restrictive, and prices are reasonable compared to market values.

2.  **Query:** अनुबंध को फिर से निविदा करने से पहले खरीद इकाई को क्या जांचना चाहिए?
    **Answer:** अनुबंध को फिर से निविदा करने से पहले, खरीद इकाई को यह जांचना चाहिए कि पूछताछ जारी करते समय सभी आवश्यक आवश्यकताएं और औपचारिकताएं, जैसे मानक शर्तें, उद्योग-अनुकूल विनिर्देश, व्यापक प्रचार, बोली के लिए पर्याप्त समय आदि, पूरी की गई थीं।

3. गैर-तर्कसंगत / विभागीय दर से अधिक की निविदा का निस्तारण – गैर-तर्कसंगत निविदा के निस्तारण के सम्बन्ध में UP Procurement of Goods-2016 के प्रस्तर-14.20 में निम्नलिखित प्रावधान किया गया है–

**प्रस्तर-14.20**
If L1’s price is not reasonable, the following steps are to be followed:
*   In the first place, the purchase organisation is to review its own data and details to recheck whether the reasonable price so arrived is correct or not.
*   If it is correct, the purchase organisation may, strictly as an exception, negotiate the price only with the lowest evaluated responsive tender (L1) in an attempt to bring down the same.
*   If L1 reduces the price to the desired (reasonable) level, a contract may be placed on it.
*   If L1 does not agree, then further action like re-tendering etc. may be decided by the purchase organisation depending on the merits of the case.

4. बराबर दरों वाली निविदा का निस्तारण – बराबर दरों वाली निविदा के निस्तारण के सम्बन्ध में Manual for Procurement of Works-2022 भारत सरकार के प्रस्तर-5.6.5 में निम्नलिखित प्रावधान किये गये है–

**प्रस्तर-5.6.5**
It is possible that sometimes a group of bidders quote the same rate against a tender. Such pool/ cartel formation is against the basic principle of competitive bidding and defeats the very purpose of an open and competitive tendering system.
*   Such and similar tactics to avoid/ control true competition in a tender leading to "appreciable adverse effect on competition" have been declared as an offence under the Competition Act, 2002, as amended by the Competition (Amendment) Act, 2007.
*   Such practices should be severely discouraged with strong measures.
*   In case of evidence of cartel formation, detailed cost analysis may be done by associating experts if necessary.
*   Besides, suitable administrative actions can be resorted to, such as:
    *   Rejecting the offers.
    *   Reporting the matter to trade associations.
    *   Reporting the matter to the Competition Commission etc.
    *   Requesting them, inter-alia, to take suitable strong actions against such firms.
*   New firms may also be encouraged to get themselves enlisted for the subject goods to break the monopolistic attitude of the existing firms.

#### संभावी प्रश्नोत्तर

**Query 1 (English):** What is the process for handling tenders where L1's price is not reasonable, as per UP Procurement of Goods-2016, paragraph 14.20?
**Answer:** The purchase organisation must first review its own data to recheck the reasonable price. If confirmed, they may negotiate with L1 (as an exception) to reduce the price. If L1 agrees, the contract is placed; if not, further actions like re-tendering are decided based on the case's merits.

**Query 2 (Hindi):** बराबर दरों वाली निविदाओं के निस्तारण के संबंध में Manual for Procurement of Works-2022 के प्रस्तर-5.6.5 के अनुसार क्या प्रावधान हैं?
