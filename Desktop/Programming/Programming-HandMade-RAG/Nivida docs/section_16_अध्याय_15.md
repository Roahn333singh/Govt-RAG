1.  प्रस्तर-434 (माप पुस्तिका)
    Payments for all work done which is susceptible of measurement and for all supplies are made on the basis of measurements recorded in measurement-books, in accordance with the rules in Para 435. The measurement-books should, therefore, be considered as **very important account records**. All the books belonging to a division should be **numbered serially** and a **register of them should be maintained in Form No. 92** in the divisional office showing the serial number of each book, the names of the sub-divisions to which issued, the date of issue and the date of its return, so that its eventual return to the divisional office may be watched.

    **Notes:**
    *   (1) A similar register should also be maintained in the sub-divisional office showing the names of the sub-divisional officer and sectional officers to whom measurement-books are issued. Books no longer in the use should be withdrawn promptly even though not completely written up.
    *   (2) Works or supplies costing below certain thresholds need not be entered in the measurement-books:
        *   **Below Rs. 50/-**: In the departmental construction units for bridges and buildings in the Public Works Department.
        *   **Below Rs. 10/-**: In other cases which are debitable to "Establishment Contingencies", "Tools and Plant", "Works" and "Repairs".
    *   (3) Medicines and medical stores supplied to canal dispensaries should not be entered in measurement-books, but should be entered in Dispensary Form No. 38, referred to in Para 1019, Chapter XXIII, Part III of the Uttar Pradesh Medical Manual.
    *   (4) The supply of electricity to Government buildings need not be entered in the measurement books but it should be entered in a separate register maintained for the purpose.

**Query:** What are the requirements for maintaining measurement-books in a divisional office?
**Answer:** All measurement-books belonging to a division should be numbered serially and a register of them should be maintained in Form No. 92 in the divisional office. ### 3. प्रस्तर—436
(a) Detailed measurements may be dispensed with in the case of periodical repairs when the quantities are recorded in efficiently maintained standard measurement-books, referred to in Para 437.
(b) Detailed measurements are also dispensed with in the case of works executed on lump sum contracts. See Paras 405, 406 and 534 to 547.

### 4. प्रस्तर—437 (मानक माप पुस्तिकाएँ)
It is usual in the department to maintain standard measurement-books of buildings in order to facilitate the preparation of estimates for periodical repairs. Where such standard books are maintained, it is also permissible to utilize them for the purpose of preparing contractor's bill for such repairs, so that it may not be necessary to take detailed measurements on each occasion, vide Para 436.

The following precautions should be observed in regard to the maintenance and use of standard measurement-books:

*   **(i)** The measurement-books used as standard books should be **numbered in an alphabetical series** so that the numbers may be readily distinguished from those assigned to the ordinary books wherein detailed measurements are recorded whenever work is actually done.
*   **(ii)** The entries of measurements (and abstracts thereof) in the standard books should be **recorded legibly in ink, and certified as correct by a responsible officer.**
*   **(iii)** An assurance should be obtained periodically of the divisional officer that all the standard books of the division have been inspected by him, that the entries therein have not been tampered with, and that all corrections due to additions and alterations in the buildings have been made in the books and the latter are reliable and up-to-date records.
*   **(iv)** When a payment is based on standard measurements, the **gazetted officer or subordinate preparing the bill for payment should be required to certify that the whole of the work** (or work since previous running bill, as the case may be) as per standard measurements **has been done and that it has not previously been billed for in any shape.**

#### संभावी प्रश्नोत्तर

**1. प्रश्न: मानक माप पुस्तिकाओं के रखरखाव और उपयोग के संबंध में कौन सी मुख्य सावधानियां बरतनी चाहिए?**
**उत्तर:** मानक माप पुस्तिकाएं एक वर्णानुक्रमिक श्रृंखला में क्रमांकित होनी चाहिए, प्रविष्टियाँ स्याही में सुपाठ्य रूप से दर्ज की जानी चाहिए और एक जिम्मेदार अधिकारी द्वारा सही प्रमाणित की जानी चाहिए, तथा विभागीय अधिकारी से यह सुनिश्चित करने का आवधिक आश्वासन प्राप्त किया जाना चाहिए कि पुस्तकों का निरीक्षण किया गया है और उनमें कोई छेड़छाड़ नहीं हुई है।

**2. Query: Under what circumstances can detailed measurements be dispensed with according to Para 436?**
**Answer:** Detailed measurements can be dispensed with in cases of periodical repairs where quantities are recorded in efficiently maintained standard measurement-books, and for works executed on lump sum contracts.

**5. प्रस्तर-438 (खण्डीय लेखाकार द्वारा मापन की प्रविष्टि की जाँच करना)**
*   उप-विभागीय अधिकारियों को समय-समय पर उपयोग में लाई जा रही मापन-पुस्तिका को संभागीय कार्यालय में प्रस्तुत करने के लिए अपेक्षित किया जाएगा, ताकि वर्ष में कम से कम एक बार प्रत्येक पुस्तिका में दर्ज प्रविष्टियों की संभागीय लेखाकार द्वारा **10 प्रतिशत की प्रतिशत जाँच** की जा सके।
*   यह जाँच संभागीय अधिकारी के पर्यवेक्षण में होगी।
*   रिकॉर्ड किए गए मापों की जांच के संबंध में विस्तृत निर्देश विभाग की संबंधित नियमावली में निहित हैं।

**6. प्रस्तर-439 (अधीक्षण अभियंता द्वारा माप पुस्तिकाओं का अनुश्रवण)**
*   अधीक्षण अभियंता को अपने दौरों के दौरान यह सुनिश्चित करना उनका विशेष कर्तव्य है कि मापन पुस्तिकाएँ सावधानीपूर्वक रखी जाती हैं और मापन ठीक से दर्ज किया जाता है।
*   यह भी सुनिश्चित करना उनका कर्तव्य है कि जिन कार्यों के लिए प्रमाण पत्र जारी किए गए हैं, उनके वास्तविक मापों के पूर्ण रिकॉर्ड उपलब्ध हैं।
*   जाँच मापों से संबंधित निर्देशों का विधिवत पालन किया जाता है।

**7. आई.एम.ओ. प्रस्तर-231 (1) (मापन की जाँच)**
*   मापन पुस्तिकाएँ सभी खातों का आधार होती हैं।
*   कार्यकारी अभियंता का यह कर्तव्य है कि वे यह देखें कि मुद्रित निर्देशों, विशेषकर फ्लाई-लीफ पर दिए गए और वित्तीय हैंडबुक, खंड VI, अध्याय XIV में दिए गए निर्देशों का सख्ती से पालन किया जाता है।
*   उन्हें स्वयं कुछ जाँच माप करने चाहिए और स्वयं को संतुष्ट करना चाहिए कि उनके कर्मचारी नियमों का पालन करते हैं।
*   उनके द्वारा की गई सभी जाँचों के संबंध में एक नोट संबंधित मापन पुस्तिकाओं में दर्ज किया जाना चाहिए।
*   यहाँ तक कि उन मामलों में भी जहाँ वह टेप से वास्तव में जाँच नहीं करता है, उसे अपने द्वारा निरीक्षण किए गए कार्यों के मापों की स्वीकृति का एक नोट बनाना चाहिए।

            *   Secures a lien on the material.
            *   Is safeguarded against losses due to the contractor postponing the execution of the work or to the shortage or misuse of the materials.
            *   Is safeguarded against the expense entailed for their proper watch and safe custody.
        *   **Payment Certification:** Payment of such advances should be made only on the certificate of an officer, confirming that:
            *   The quantities of materials upon which the advances are made have actually been brought to site.
            *   The contractor has not previously received any advance on that security.
            *   The materials are all required by the contractor for use on items of work for which rates for finished work have been agreed upon.
