All superintending Engineer, Irrigation Department.

In clauses 2(a) and 2(b) of the conditions of contract I.D. Form No.111- it has been laid down that if a contractor does not finish the works by a stipulated date, he shall be liable to pay as compensation, penalty not exceeding 10% of the estimated cost of the work as shown in the tender. In clause 3 of the same conditions of contract, it has been laid down that in any case in which under any clause or clauses of his contract, the contractor shall have rendered himself liable to pay compensation amounting to the whole of his security deposit. The executive Engineer, shall have power to adopt any of the three courses as mentioned in Sub-Clauses (a), (b) and (c) of the clause 3 which briefly state:

(i) Rescission of the contract and forfeiture of the whole of contractors deposit.
(ii) Measuring up of the work done by the contractor and then employing departmental labour to complete it and debiting the contractor with the actual cost.
(iii) Measuring up the work done by the contractor and then giving the remaining work for completion by another contractor, after taking it out from the hands of the original contractor and debiting the extra cost to the latters account.

2.  Before adopting any of the three courses, the Deptt. has tor serve the contractor with a properly worded notice. It has been seen that the form of communication in this regard made by the officers of the Department sometime contain faulty or defective language, which in the event of the contractor going to a court of law places the Department in an embarrassing position.
    Necessity for properly wording such communication to contractors has to be realised by all officers, For their guidance and necessary action 3 Draft notices to cover the provisions of clause 3 of the Conditions of contract I.D. Form No. 111 are enclosed herewith. The language of these draft is approved by the Law Department of the Uttar Pradesh Govt. and these should be adopted by all concerned in future.

## संभावी प्रश्नोत्तर (Contextual Q&A for RAG)

1.  **प्रश्न:** ठेकेदार द्वारा अनुबंधित कार्य पूर्ण न करने पर सक्षम स्तर द्वारा ID Form-111 के प्रस्तर-3(अ) में कौन-कौन से प्रमुख प्रावधान किए गए हैं?
    **उत्तर:** ठेकेदार द्वारा अनुबंधित कार्य पूर्ण न करने पर ID Form-111 के प्रस्तर-3(अ) में तीन प्रमुख प्रावधान किए गए हैं: अनुबंध को निरस्त किया जाना, ठेकेदार के अनुबंध के सापेक्ष विभाग द्वारा कार्य पूर्ण कराया जाना, और ठेकेदार के अनुबंध के सापेक्ष किसी अन्य ठेकेदार से कार्य पूर्ण कराया जाना।

2.  **Question:** What are the three courses of action an Executive Engineer can adopt as per Sub-Clauses (a), (b), and (c) of Clause 3 of ID Form No. 111 conditions of contract, when a contractor fails to complete work?
    **Answer:** The three courses of action are: (i) Rescission of the contract and forfeiture of the whole of the contractor's deposit, (ii) Measuring up the work done by the contractor and then employing departmental labour to complete it and debiting the contractor with the actual cost, and (iii) Measuring up the work done by the contractor and then giving the remaining work for completion to another contractor, after taking it out from the hands of the original contractor and debiting the extra cost to the latter's account.

3.  **प्रश्न:** यदि कोई ठेकेदार निर्धारित तिथि तक कार्य पूरा नहीं करता है, तो उसे अनुमानित लागत के कितने प्रतिशत से अधिक का मुआवजा नहीं देना होगा?
    **उत्तर:** यदि कोई ठेकेदार निर्धारित तिथि तक कार्य पूरा नहीं करता है, तो उसे दंड के रूप में कार्य की अनुमानित लागत के 10% से अधिक का मुआवजा नहीं देना होगा जैसा कि टेंडर में दिखाया गया है।

4.  **Question:** What is the purpose of the three draft notices enclosed with Memo No. 8135/IRD/89 B-132 W dated Dec. 20, 1960?
    **Answer:** The three draft notices are enclosed to cover the provisions of clause 3 of the Conditions of contract I.D. Form No. 111, for the guidance and necessary action of officers. These standardized drafts, approved by the Law Department of the Uttar Pradesh Govt., aim to prevent faulty or defective language in communications which could embarrass the Department in court.

5.  **प्रश्न:** अनुबंध निरस्त करने के नोटिस (Under Clause 3(1)(a)) के तहत, यदि ठेकेदार शर्तों का पालन करने में विफल रहता है, तो सरकार को हुए नुकसान की भरपाई के लिए क्या कार्रवाई की जाती है?
    **उत्तर:** यदि ठेकेदार शर्तों का पालन करने में विफल रहता है, तो अनुबंध Clause 3(1)(a) के तहत निरस्त कर दिया जाता है, और ठेकेदार की पूरी सुरक्षा जमा राशि को सरकार द्वारा हुए नुकसान की भरपाई के लिए जब्त कर लिया जाता है।
