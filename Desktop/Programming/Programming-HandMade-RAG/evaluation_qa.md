### 📂 Source: `1.Establishment / APPOINTMENT PROBATION CONFIMATION PROMOTION PPT.pdf`

**11. Question:** What is the standard duration of a probation period for a government servant, and what is the maximum it can be extended?
**Expected Answer:** The standard probation period is 2 years. It can be extended by up to an additional 1 year in exceptional cases, making the maximum total probation period 3 years.

**12. Question:** Under what circumstances is a government servant "deemed" to be suspended?
**Expected Answer:** A government servant is deemed to be suspended when they are detained in custody for a period exceeding 48 hours, or when they are convicted and sentenced to imprisonment by a court.

**13. Question:** What is the constitutional basis for a government servant's tenure?
**Expected Answer:** A government servant holds office at the pleasure of the President (or Governor at the state level) under Article 310 of the Indian Constitution, subject to the procedural safeguards provided under Article 311.

### 📂 Source: `1.Establishment / Disciplinary Proceeding Rule- 1999 PPT.pdf`

**14. Question:** What are the primary reasons for which disciplinary action can be initiated against a government servant?
**Expected Answer:** Disciplinary action can be initiated for misconduct, negligence of duty, corruption, misappropriation of government funds, insubordination, or conduct unbecoming of a government servant.

**15. Question:** What is the difference between a "Minor Penalty" and a "Major Penalty" in disciplinary proceedings?
**Expected Answer:** Minor penalties include censure, withholding of increments/promotion, recovery from pay, and reduction to a lower grade temporarily. Major penalties include compulsory retirement, removal from service, and dismissal from service.

### 📂 Source: `5.Flood / CWC DPR_Guidelines_Flood_management_Works_12Apr2018.pdf`

**22. Question:** What does DPR stand for in the context of flood management works?
**Expected Answer:** DPR stands for Detailed Project Report. It is a comprehensive document that outlines the technical and financial aspects of a proposed flood management project before it is submitted for approval and funding.

**23. Question:** What are the key components that must be included in a Detailed Project Report (DPR) for flood management works?
**Expected Answer:** A DPR for flood management works typically must include a project introduction, hydrological analysis, design details for embankments/structures, cost estimates, economic analysis (Benefit-Cost ratio), environmental impact assessment, and implementation schedule.

### 📂 Source: `6.Canals / Guidelines for Canal Lining.pdf`

**24. Question:** Why is canal lining done? What are its main benefits?
**Expected Answer:** Canal lining is done to: (1) prevent seepage loss of water, thereby increasing irrigation efficiency; (2) prevent waterlogging in adjacent agricultural land; (3) reduce maintenance costs; (4) control weed growth; and (5) allow higher flow velocity without causing erosion.

**25. Question:** What are the standard types of canal lining materials specified in the guidelines?
**Expected Answer:** The standard lining materials include brick lining, stone lining, plain cement concrete (PCC) lining, and precast concrete slab lining. The choice depends on the canal size, soil conditions, and material availability.

**26. Question:** What is the minimum grade of concrete specified for reinforced concrete (RCC) construction in general structural work?
**Expected Answer:** As per IS 456, the minimum grade of concrete for RCC construction in general structural work is M20 (characteristic compressive strength of 20 N/mm²).

**27. Question:** What is the minimum cement content specified for moderate exposure conditions in RCC?
**Expected Answer:** For moderate exposure conditions, IS 456 specifies a minimum cement content of 300 kg/m³ with a maximum water-cement ratio of 0.50.

### 📂 Source: `2.Revenue / NI Canal Act by Baburam.pdf`

**28. Question:** Under the Northern India Canal and Drainage Act, what are the rights of the government regarding the acquisition of land for canal construction?
**Expected Answer:** The government has the right to enter, survey, and acquire land needed for the construction, maintenance, or improvement of canals. Compensation is to be provided to the landowners as per the applicable land acquisition laws.

**29. Question:** What is the penalty for obstructing or damaging a government canal or irrigation channel?
**Expected Answer:** Under the NI Canal Act, damaging or obstructing a canal is a criminal offense and is punishable with a fine and/or imprisonment as specified in the Act.

**30. Question:** What is the purpose of a 'drain' in the context of the irrigation department?
**Expected Answer:** A drain (or drainage channel) is used to carry away excess water from agricultural fields, remove waterlogging, and manage the water table. The Irrigation Department is responsible for the maintenance and construction of drainage systems to protect agricultural land.
